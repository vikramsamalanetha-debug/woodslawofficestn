About the Firm

Serving West Tennessee since 1982

Wood Law Offices was established in Parsons in 1982 and later opened a second office in Lexington.

For more than four decades, the firm has represented clients in Decatur County, Henderson County and the surrounding area. Clients work directly with their attorney, and consultations are available at either office.
