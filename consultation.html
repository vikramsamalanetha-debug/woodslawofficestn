Before You Request

Common questions

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit?

No. Your preferred time is subject to office confirmation. The office will contact you to confirm it or offer another time.

What should I bring?

Bring photo identification and any documents related to your matter, such as deeds, court papers, letters or prior wills.

What should I include in the form?

A short description is enough. Please do not include confidential details. Submitting a request does not create an attorney–client relationship.

My matter is urgent.

Call the Parsons office at (731) 847-6713 or the Lexington office at (731) 968-9908.
